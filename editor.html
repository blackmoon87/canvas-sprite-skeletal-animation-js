🛠 Character Rig Editor

← Back to the demo

1. Load your character

Required pose: a biped (2 arms, 2 legs) standing in an A-pose: arms held away from the body, legs apart, hands and feet clearly visible, with a gap between each limb and the body. Best: a turnaround from front (0°) through right side (90°) to back (180°). One front or side image also works.

2. Auto-rig

Places every joint from the silhouette. Then fine-tune by dragging.

3. Joints

Drag the dots. Front view sets left/right and height, side view sets depth (forward/back) and height. The yellow line is the floor (lowest heel / toe). Views start at front (A) and side (B); their sliders pick any frame.

Clean the sprite

          
Remove leftover background, shadows, watermarks or noise. Work in the front or side view;
            use each view's frame slider to reach any angle.

          

            
            
            
            
            
            
          

          
          
          
          
          
          
          
Tip for busy photos / video frames: set Background to "none", draw a border around the
            character with ⭕, then press 🧹. Fix the rest with the wand, eraser and restore brushes.

          

            
            
          

        

        

          
Body parts

          
Fix which pixels move with which bone: pick a part, then paint or draw a border around it.
            For an arm or leg, the border is its exact outline (everything else goes back to the body).
            Colors: head, torso,
            arm L, arm R,
            leg L, leg R,
            front cloth, back cloth.
