🛠 Character Rig Editor

← Back to the demo

1. Load your character

A biped (2 arms, 2 legs) standing in a neutral pose. Best: a turnaround from front (0°) through right side (90°) to back (180°). One front or side image also works.

2. Auto-rig

Places every joint from the silhouette. Then fine-tune by dragging.

3. Joints

Drag the dots. Front view sets left/right and height, side view sets depth (forward/back) and height. The yellow line is the floor (lowest heel / toe).
